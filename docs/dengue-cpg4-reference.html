IV fluid calculation guide (CPG4) — formulas and examples

AreaImplemented ruleSource
DiagnosisSuspected/confirmed status is clinician-selected; confirmed cases require a recorded laboratory basis. Completed assessment review is required before generating a diagnosis. Include illness day, phase, DNWS/DWWS/severe dengue, shock status and comorbidities/special populations. Management groups stay outside the copied diagnosis.Table 7 p30; Recommendation 2 p31
Assessment and severityClinician-entered haemodynamic status; no shock diagnosis from HR or pulse pressure alone. Suggested A/B1/B2/C category uses the full documented assessment. Stable co-morbidity prompts consideration of referral, rather than automatic admission.Table 6 p28; Algorithms 2–3 pp xv–xvi
Warning signsInterpret near/in critical phase. Persistent vomiting is >3 episodes/day. Diarrhoea is assessed for dehydration/oral intolerance. Specific findings include intense abdominal pain/tenderness, reduced/absent urine 4–6 hours and clinician-confirmed HCT/platelet trends.Tables 3,6; pp14–15,28–30
Oral hydration and urineApproximately 200 mL/hour while awake; individualise for fluid restriction. Electrolyte-containing fluids; avoid plain water alone. Monitor urine, usually 4–6 voids/day, at least once in 6 hours and ≥0.5 mL/kg/hour where measured.pp31,34
Weight and maintenanceHolliday–Segar 4/2/1 for total oral + IV maintenance. BMI >27.5: ABW = IBW + 0.4(actual − IBW). IBW: female 45.5 or male 50 + 0.91(height cm −152). Calculation weight maximum 70 kg for maintenance/quota; not a cap on shock resuscitation.pp40–42,48
Non-shock critical-phase quotaOne day's maintenance + 50 mL/kg, across 48 hours; includes oral and IV. Not all patients need the full quota. Not applicable once shock develops. The tracker records a separately agreed target and does not prescribe the remaining balance.pp43–46; Appendix 11 p114
Initial B2 fluidWhen IV is clinically needed, 3–5 mL/kg/hour, reassess after 1 hour using Algorithm 1. Adequate oral intake may remove the need for IV; p44 describes tolerated oral intake equivalent to 1–2 mL/kg/hour. Oral tolerance alone does not confirm adequacy: the calculator requests clinician assessment when tolerance is preserved and no dehydration is recorded. Group B2 requires hospital admission.pp28,44,46
Initial shock fluidCompensated: crystalloid 10 mL/kg over 1 hour. Decompensated: 20 mL/kg over 15–30 minutes. Immediate reassessment and emergency transfer; subsequent fluids are not automatically prescribed.Algorithms 2–3
Medication reviewSGLT2 sick-day withholding; metformin/statin continuation only without contraindications; individual review of antiplatelet/anticoagulant and chronic steroid indications. Avoid NSAIDs/steroids as dengue treatment. Avoid/discontinue paracetamol with deranged liver enzymes.pp32–34
VaccinationRecord status/date. Within 30 days, prefer RT-qPCR; NS1 may be used if unavailable. IgM/IgG are not standalone confirmatory tests; positive PCR/NS1 may also reflect vaccine-derived virus.p21; Table 7 p29
Outpatient follow-up and stopping FBCDaily review until afebrile 24–48 hours without antipyretics, with clinical reassessment. Stopping routine FBC requires an explicit clinician decision after reviewing recovery and laboratory trends.pp30–31; discharge guidance p84

Interpretation points made visible in the calculator

B2 duration: text p44 permits 1–2 hours; Algorithm 1 p46 shows reassessment after 1 hour. The interface uses the algorithm's reassessment point and displays the text range. It does not automatically repeat or continue the infusion.

Quota table versus formula: at 65 kg the exact formula gives 5,770 mL while the printed table gives 5,700 mL; at the 70 kg cap it gives 6,140 mL while the table gives 6,000 mL. Both are shown when applicable. No rounding/interpolation rule is invented. Record the clinician-agreed target separately.

Primary care: quota estimates support assessment and the transfer fluid record. They are not outpatient 48-hour infusion orders. Stabilise before/during transfer, inform the receiving hospital and send the relevant records (p30).

Use of decision support

Suggested categories and consistency checks assist documentation. The clinician confirms the diagnosis, phase, treatment and disposition. A recorded override remains possible, with a rationale. Blank fields do not establish that a clinical finding is absent. This release is not a certification that every possible clinical scenario has been validated.
