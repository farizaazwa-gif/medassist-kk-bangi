GDM Risk & When to Perform MGTT

Based on the MOH Handbook of Obstetrics Guideline 2024 and the Malaysian CPG Management of Diabetes in Pregnancy 2017. At-risk women should have a 75-g mOGTT/MGTT at booking or as early as possible; if normal, repeat at 24–28 weeks. Universal screening at 24–28 weeks is recommended.
GDM risk factors
BMI >27 kg/m² is added automatically as a risk factor.
Enter gestation, BMI, previous test status and applicable risk factors.
Diagnostic reference for the Malaysian 75-g two-point OGTT: GDM if fasting plasma glucose ≥5.1 mmol/L or 2-hour plasma glucose ≥7.8 mmol/L. Overt diabetes thresholds require separate clinical confirmation and management.
